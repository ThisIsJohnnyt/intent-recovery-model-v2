PRODUCT-OWNER SPOT CHECK - 10 examples
========================================================================

You are NOT running the review checklist. Claude and Gemini both do that
adequately. What neither can do is judge these three things:

  1. Does the INPUT read like something a real person actually typed?
     Both models are guessing at what real messiness looks like. You are
     about to have a folder of genuine notes. If one of these reads as
     invented, say so - that is the failure mode that nearly got past us.

  2. If this were your note, is the NARRATIVE what you would want back?
     Not "is it defensible against the rules" - is it useful. An output can
     satisfy every rule and still be worse than the raw note.

  3. Is there anything here worth learning? Some narratives may be
     technically clean and pedagogically empty.

at random from examples edited during the 2026-08-23 session - rewritten by
