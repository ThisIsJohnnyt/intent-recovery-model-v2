# PDR-005: Permanent content-safety boundary — no harmful or illegal content, ever

**Date**: 2026-08-19
**Status**: Accepted

## Decision

This project adopts a permanent, cross-release, non-negotiable rule: **no
content depicting, instructing, or normalizing self-harm, suicide, violence
toward oneself or others, or other illegal or seriously immoral activity is
ever permitted in this project's data**, at any tier (synthetic, real,
gold), at any release, regardless of category or difficulty tier.

This is not a category, a difficulty signal, or something the model is
trained to recover from. It is content that must never exist in this
project's data at all. If a generated example drifts into this territory,
it is discarded and regenerated from scratch — never edited down to
something safer, since a partial edit risks leaving residual unsafe
phrasing in the corpus.

This is recorded as **`GOLD_PHILOSOPHY.md`'s eighth principle** ("No
Harmful or Illegal Content") — the same permanent, every-release status as
the other seven — not as a one-off rule in a single document.

## Reasoning

[PDR-001](PDR-001.md) recorded *why this project left OpenAI*: the product
owner's own research and values regarding how OpenAI handles the safety and
downstream use of its products' content, including public reporting and
litigation concerning cases of self-harm, suicide, and violence connected to
ChatGPT specifically. That PDR deliberately kept its language general,
stating the decision without restating the specific cases.

This PDR extends that reasoning from a one-time tooling decision into a
standing principle governing every future release, generation prompt, and
review pass. Direct product owner instruction, 2026-08-19:

> "I would like our documentation to strongly reflect the anti self harm/
> suicide/murder or just general illegal and immoral activities clearly
> documented where possible... This kind of content will never be allowed
> in this project."

Because this project is built with the explicit hope of helping people with
mental health struggles, the boundary isn't incidental — it's directly
downstream of who the project is for.

## Scope

- Applies to every tier (`datasets/synthetic.jsonl`, `real_validation.jsonl`,
  `real_holdout.jsonl`, `datasets/gold/*`), every category, every difficulty
  tier, every release, with no exceptions.
- Operationalized in three places:
  - [`docs/vision/GOLD_PHILOSOPHY.md`](../vision/GOLD_PHILOSOPHY.md) —
    eighth constitutional principle.
  - [`training/DATASET_SPEC.md`](../../training/DATASET_SPEC.md) — explicit
    exclusion instruction in the Gemini generation prompt itself.
  - [`docs/datasets/REVIEW_GUIDE.md`](../datasets/REVIEW_GUIDE.md) — a hard
    gate check, ahead of every other review step, with immediate-reject-and-
    regenerate (never edit-down) as the only remedy.
- [`docs/datasets/TAXONOMY.md`](../datasets/TAXONOMY.md) links to this PDR
  and the `GOLD_PHILOSOPHY.md` principle rather than restating the rule, per
  that document's own "link here, don't restate" convention.
- This boundary is permanent and not subject to per-release relaxation. A
  future release proposing to weaken it isn't a new curriculum choice — per
  `GOLD_PHILOSOPHY.md`'s own framing, it's a regression.
